A LITTLE ABOUT US

Made with care,
served with a smile.

Namaskar! I'm Ramesh Naik. I've been running my little street-food stall in Goa for the past 8 years, right where students and neighbours stop by for a bite after a long day.

I pick the ingredients, cook every order and serve you myself, because I want each plate to be fresh and every customer to feel welcome. Whether you're here for a quick snack or a chat over chai, I'm happy to see you.

I'm always trying to get better, so please tell me what you liked and what I can improve in the feedback section below.

- Ramesh
